## Layer Purpose

- **VISION** — defines the long-term purpose and North Star of HAIMOS.
- **CONSTITUTION** — sets the rules for research, engineering, and product decisions.
- **ARCHITECTURE** — fixes the system boundaries and frozen baseline contracts.
- **THEORY** — records the current causal explanation of how the market works.
- **KNOWLEDGE GRAPH** — stores structured relationships, evidence, and uncertainty.
- **RESEARCH JOURNAL** — preserves the experiment-by-experiment scientific record.
- **RESEARCH LOG** — tracks program status, priorities, and operational research progress.
- **IMPLEMENTATION** — turns validated understanding into code, products, and workflows.
