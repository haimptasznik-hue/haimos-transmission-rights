# Project Constitution

## Mission

HAIMOS exists to turn Australian Transmission Rights into an institutional-grade market physics and investment intelligence platform. Its purpose is to improve capital allocation by converting market understanding into explainable, reproducible investment decisions.

## Operating Philosophy

HAIMOS is built from first principles. The platform must understand the market before it predicts the market, and it must preserve point-in-time integrity, causal reasoning, and scientific discipline in every layer.

## Research Doctrine

One mechanism per research cycle. Pre-register hypotheses. Reject weak laws. Update the Knowledge Graph only from evidence. Never optimise models before understanding mechanisms.

## Engineering Doctrine

Use small commits. Make one logical change at a time. Protect Digital Twin integrity. Test before merge. Never bypass reproducibility or auditability.

## Product Doctrine

The product must explain every recommendation, value every SRA, model portfolio cashflow, and preserve a complete chain from physical drivers to investment decision.

## Investment Doctrine

The system must answer what to buy, why to buy it, how much to buy, when to buy it, and what happens after buying it. Capital allocation is a lifecycle, not a point estimate.

## Definition of Success

Success is measured by better investment decisions, higher risk-adjusted returns, capital efficiency, explainability, reproducibility, scientific validation, and institutional trust.

## North Star

Every experiment, every model, every line of code, and every product feature must demonstrably improve the quality of an investment decision.
