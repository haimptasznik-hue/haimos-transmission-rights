THE REAL PROBLEM:

The +$22.3M result is not a single strong signal. It's the sum of:
  • NSW1-QLD1 strong edge:  +$58.3M
  • V-SA strong edge:        +$17.0M
  • VIC1-NSW1 failure:       -$53.0M
  • Net result:              +$22.3M

If the VIC1-NSW1 losses had been -$35M instead of -$53M, total would be +$40M.

The strategy's viability hinges on whether -$53M is an anomaly or a systematic problem.
