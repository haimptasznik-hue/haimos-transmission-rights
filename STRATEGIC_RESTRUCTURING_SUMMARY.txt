RESEARCH FRAMEWORK: Arrow-by-Arrow Validation
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Each arrow = one testable causal relationship

Example: ARROW_001 — Temperature → Regional Demand

Process:
1. Design (Pre-register criteria BEFORE code):
   • Does temperature drive demand?
   • Acceptance: ρ > 0.5 in NSW1, VIC1, SA1
   • Rejection: ρ < 0.2 all regions OR reversal by season
   • Data: C2024Q4 DISPATCHREGIONSUM + BOM temp (PIT-safe)

2. Implement (Agent runs code):
   • Load data
   • Compute correlation by region
   • Test lagged effects
   • Output: CSV + markdown result

3. Analyze (You as Chief Analyst):
   • Challenge result: Holds across ALL subsamples?
   • Boundary conditions: When does it hold/break?
   • Residuals: XX% of variance unexplained (why?)
   • Record in Research Journal

4. Record (Update central models):
   • Add LAW to MARKET_PHYSICS_MODEL.md
   • Mark entry in Research Journal index
   • Decide next arrow

Result: Every arrow is documented, every relationship is quantified,
        every conclusion is pre-registered and testable.
