# VISION

Every experiment, every model, every line of code, and every product feature must demonstrably improve the quality of an investment decision.

## 1. Mission

HAIMOS exists to turn Australian Transmission Rights into an intelligible, decision-grade asset class. The market is shaped by physical constraints, operational state, settlement rules, and changing portfolio conditions, yet the available tooling is still fragmented, descriptive, and built for retrospective analysis rather than institutional decision-making. HAIMOS is being built to provide a Bloomberg-quality intelligence platform for the Australian SRA market: one that unifies market understanding, valuation, portfolio construction, execution context, and performance attribution into a single system that supports serious capital allocation.

## 2. End State

The completed platform is not a price forecaster. It is an institutional decision-support system that discovers opportunities, values every SRA, recommends investments, explains every recommendation, models portfolio cashflow, tracks realised performance, and continuously learns from market outcomes. It should allow an investor to move from market observation to capital commitment with a defensible, reproducible, and auditable chain of reasoning.

## 3. Core Philosophy

HAIMOS is built from first principles. The hierarchy is:

External Physical Drivers
↓

↓

Forecasting is only one layer in this stack. Understanding the market comes before predicting the market.

## 4. Competitive Advantage

Traditional quantitative systems learn statistical relationships. HAIMOS is designed to discover physical mechanisms. That difference matters because every recommendation must be explainable, and every investment must have a causal narrative that survives scrutiny from researchers, investors, and institutional customers. The goal is not to fit history more closely; it is to understand why the market behaves as it does and to translate that understanding into better capital decisions.

## 5. Investment Intelligence

The platform must ultimately answer five questions:

1. What should I buy?
2. Why should I buy it?
3. How much should I buy?
4. When should I buy it?
5. What happens after I buy it?

The fifth question is essential. A complete investment lifecycle must be understood end to end:

Auction
↓
Capital committed
↓
Capital locked
↓
Settlement schedule
↓
Cashflow generation
↓
Portfolio return
↓
Capital released
↓
Reinvestment

## 6. Success Metrics

Success is not measured solely by prediction accuracy, R², or correlation. Those metrics are useful only insofar as they improve investment outcomes. The real measures of success are better investment decisions, higher risk-adjusted returns, capital efficiency, explainability, reproducibility, scientific validation, and institutional trust.

## 7. Research Doctrine

The operating doctrine is simple: one mechanism per research cycle. Pre-register hypotheses. Reject weak laws. Update the Knowledge Graph only from evidence. Never optimise models before understanding mechanisms. Knowledge before models. Models before decisions.

## 9. The Ultimate Product

HAIMOS is building the world's first institutional-grade Market Physics and Investment Intelligence platform for Australian Transmission Rights. Its purpose is not merely to predict prices, but to understand the physical mechanisms that create them, convert that understanding into investment intelligence, and optimise the complete lifecycle of capital allocation.
